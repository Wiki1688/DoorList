## The user and the job

> A registration-desk volunteer at a 200-person student conference opens this screen to check attendees in as they arrive and flag anyone whose payment is unconfirmed, and knows it worked when the checked-in counter matches the queue in front of them and nobody flagged has a badge.

**User type:** B, internal. Two or three volunteers on their phones at the entrance table during a 45-minute arrival rush.
**Business function augmented:** event operations, registration. The step is owned today by the registration lead, who works from a printed attendee list, a highlighter, and a WhatsApp thread to the treasurer for "did this person pay?" questions.

## What the app does

Two screens, no page reloads, all data invented.

**Desk** - the volunteer's screen. Search by name or organisation; a list of attendees showing name, organisation, ticket type (Standard / Student / Speaker) and payment status (Paid / Unconfirmed); filter chips *All · Not arrived · Checked in · Flagged*, with the chosen filter remembered when the page is closed and reopened; a counter reading *Checked in X / Y*.

- Tapping a **Paid** attendee checks them in (row greys out, tick appears). Tapping again undoes it.
- Tapping an **Unconfirmed** attendee does **not** check them in. It opens a confirmation, *Payment confirmed at desk?*, with *Confirmed - check in* and *Cancel*. The volunteer decides; the screen only records the decision.
- Add, edit and delete attendee controls were added during the build (Prompts 7 and 8) for walk-ins and corrections. Their trade-offs are discussed in REFLECTION.md Q3 and Q4.

**Summary** - the registration lead's screen. Three large numbers (checked in, expected, flagged unconfirmed), a check-in progress bar, and the list of flagged attendees, readable from two metres away.

## What is invented

Every name, organisation, ticket and payment status in `src/data/attendees.ts` is made up. No real person, company, university or event appears. The app calls no model and no outside service; it runs entirely on the data file. Check-in state is kept in the phone's own browser storage.

## How to repeat this build

1. Copy the master prompt (Prompt 1 in [PROMPTS.md](PROMPTS.md)) into **Google AI Studio → Build** (aistudio.google.com/apps) with a personal Google account, and press *Build*.
2. Check the preview against the numbered Goal list, then send the follow-up prompts in PROMPTS.md one at a time, each ending "Change nothing else."
3. In AI Studio, **Settings → GitHub → create repository** (public, under your own account), then push.
4. At **vercel.com/new**, import the repository with the same GitHub account, accept the detected settings and deploy. Use the short production URL.

## Known limitations

Check-ins live in each phone's browser, so two volunteers' phones do not share one list. The Edit form can change payment status without passing through the confirmation dialogue. On narrow phones the *Flagged* chip may need a sideways scroll. Each is named, with what I would do about it, in REFLECTION.md.
